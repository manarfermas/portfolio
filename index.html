⬡ About Me

Empowering the Next Generation Through Technology

I'm Manar Fermas, a STEAM Educator, Software Engineer and Training Manager from Algeria, passionate about transforming complex technology into engaging learning experiences for children and young learners.

Holding a Master's Degree in Software Engineering from the University of Constantine 2 Abdelhamid Mehri, I bridge the gap between advanced technical knowledge and effective pedagogy. My work spans robotics education, Arduino programming, STEAM curriculum design, and managing training programs that produce measurable impact.

Having taught over 100 students aged 7–17, I believe every child deserves access to quality technology education that prepares them for a rapidly evolving world. I am actively seeking international opportunities in UAE, Qatar, Europe and the United States to expand this impact globally.
